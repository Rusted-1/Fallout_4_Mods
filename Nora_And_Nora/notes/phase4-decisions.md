# Phase 4 decisions (relationship tiers 4–5, duo romance, Eternal Lovers)

Answers to the open design questions, 2026-10-23. The design itself is in `interaction-ideas.md`.

1. **Nice / mean path.** "Mean to Synth Nora" for Organic Nora's tier-4 talk = the cruel flag from tiers 1–3.
2. **Broken phase** (from "Sweetie she's just a machine" in the tier-4 mean talk). Synth Nora only gives flat
   lines like a standard synth: "Yes sir.", "Affirmative, sir." Her affinity reactions and her idle commentary stop.
3. **"You two fell in love?"** Replies are in the design file (Synth Nora, then Organic Nora).
4. **Declined duo romance** comes back after about 12 in-game days, as a greeting.
5. **Tier 5 answers:** Thank you (Organic likes), I'll withhold judgment (none), I love you too (Organic loves),
   Where are you going with this (none).
6. **Eternal Lovers** works like a vanilla companion perk: permanent once unlocked, whether or not the Noras are
   travelling with you. Each Nora +100 HP; player regenerates 1 HP/s and takes 50% less fire, cryo and poison damage.
7. **Tier timing.** A tier is reached when that tier's talk happens.
   - Tier 4: Organic Nora's talk, 4 days after tier 3 is reached.
   - Tier 5: 4 days after the tier-4 talk; 3 days with "Honestly I've come to really enjoy having her around".
   - Tier-4 mean talk, other answers (proposed, not objected to): "too hard on her" = 4 days; "I just don't know"
     = +1 day; "just a machine" = tier 5 locked, broken phase; "take her out" = hostile.
