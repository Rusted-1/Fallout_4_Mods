# Quests That Hit Synth Nora Hardest

Written 2026-10-21. Working copy: the "Quests That Hit Synth Nora Hardest" doc on claude.ai (https://claude.ai/artifact/QB446Ctd7LUx4JQ6ftuxCH).

## Why these quests

About 15 vanilla and Far Harbor quests touch synth Nora directly. The rest of the game only touches her through faction affinity. She is a synth carrying another woman's memories, so a quest hits her hardest when it is about one of four things:

- **Where she came from:** the Institute, Shaun, and the night in Vault 111 that she remembers but didn't live.
- **Synths with false or erased memories:** H2-22, Chase, Avery and DiMA all mirror her.
- **Synth hunting:** the Covenant SAFE test, coursers, the Brotherhood, Danse.
- **What the player decides about synths for good:** the four faction endings.

Organic Nora sees the same quests from the other side. She is the woman synth Nora was copied from, which suits two-voice scenes.

## Main quest

The two biggest moments for her are Dangerous Minds and Institutionalized. Each one takes away part of what she believes about herself.

- **Reunions** (Kellogg dies). Our Kellogg fix already gates her affinity here. Hook: she wanted this revenge for a life she only remembers. Organic Nora actually lived it.
- **Dangerous Minds** (Kellogg's memories in the Memory Den). The player watches Shaun being taken from the pod. Hook: she sees the real Nora shot in that memory, where she remembers being shot herself. This is the second time her memories prove false, after the Vault 111 pod. A natural tier 1–2 talk.
- **Hunter/Hunted** (killing a courser for its chip). Hook: coursers hunt synths like her. Her battle-site note already says she was hunting them.
- **Institutionalized** (meeting Father). This is the Shaun reaction in the design notes. Hook: she learns who ordered her memories. The notes say she resents Shaun but still loves him.
- **Nuclear Family** (Institute ending, Father dies). Hook: the notes say both Noras like this ending but it hurts to lose him.

## Faction quests

The design notes already cover joining, helping and ending for each faction. These are the single quests inside each line that matter most to her.

- **Railroad: Road to Freedom** (joining). Her Love moment. Hook: the first group that sees her as a person without being asked to.
- **Brotherhood: Blind Betrayal** (Danse is revealed as a synth). This is the strongest single quest for her: a synth who believed he was human, hunted by his own family. Hook: her reaction depends on the choice about Danse. Organic Nora's, at tier 3 or higher, is her promise to protect her.
- **Institute: Synth Retention** (the first job, bringing back an escaped synth). Hook: she is now one of the escapees being hunted.
- **Institute: End of the Line** (wiping out the Railroad). The notes give Synth Hate and a panic here.
- **Minutemen: With Our Powers Combined** (destroying the Brotherhood). The notes give Synth Like ("I feel a lot safer").

## Side quests

[Human Error](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Human_Error) is the side quest to build first. It puts the question "is she a synth?" in front of her as a test you cannot fail.

- **Human Error** (Covenant). Swanson gives everyone the SAFE test at the gate. In the Compound, Dr. Chambers tortures Amelia Stockton, a real synth, to tune the test. Hooks: she takes the test at the gate and passes, which unsettles her more than failing would. At the Compound, freeing Amelia is a Love and siding with Chambers is a Hate. Organic Nora, a lawyer, objects to the torture in either case.
- **[Memory Interrupted](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Memory_Interrupted)** (Railroad, Desdemona). H2-22 is a synth who chose to have Dr. Amari alter his memories. Hook: she asks whether it would be kinder to forget she was ever Nora. Organic Nora's answer can depend on the tier.
- **In Sheep's Clothing** (Diamond City, after a non-Institute ending). [Mayor McDonough](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Mayor_McDonough) is a synth replacement and takes Geneva hostage. Hook: a synth replacing a person is exactly what organic Nora feared about her in Phase 1.
- **Political Leanings** (Institute radiant, Justin Ayo). Another way to find out McDonough is a synth. Hook: a short line from each Nora.

## Far Harbor

Far Harbor is built around "am I a synth, and does it matter?", so it may be the DLC that suits her best. Building it needs DLCCoast.esm, which hasn't been copied over yet.

- **Far From Home / Where You Belong** (Kasumi Nakano). Kasumi is a human who ran away because she thinks she might be a synth. Hook: the mirror image of synth Nora. Organic Nora is the one who can talk to Kasumi.
- **The Way Life Should Be** (Captain Avery). DiMA killed the real [Captain Avery](https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Captain_Avery) and replaced her with a synth. Hook: organic Nora's worst fear from Phase 1, made real.
- **Best Left Forgotten** (DiMA's erased memories). DiMA cut out his own memories to stay who he is. Hook: synth Nora asks whether she could do the same, and what she would keep.
- **The Price of Memory** (Acadia, Chase). Chase asks you to recover memories she gave up. *Details not confirmed from a source; check in game.* Hook: the reverse of Memory Interrupted.

## Build order and the testing holotape

Quest reactions are planned as Phase 3, after the relationship tiers. Each reaction should change with the tier, so the tiers have to exist first.

1. Dangerous Minds and Institutionalized: the Shaun reaction the notes already cover.
2. Blind Betrayal and Human Error: the two strongest synth-hunting moments.
3. The four faction endings, from the notes.
4. Memory Interrupted, Road to Freedom and the Institute quests.
5. Far Harbor, once DLCCoast.esm is copied over.

**Testing holotape** ("Nora and Nora: Test Tape"). An optional installer item you tick during install; it then shows up in the inventory on load, so testing doesn't mean waiting days of game time. Menu:

- **Status:** current tier, which talks are done, and days left on the current timer.
- **Advance to next tier:** plays that tier's talk right away.
- **Set tier 1–5:** jumps straight to a tier, skipping the talks.
- **Skip current wait:** ends the 7-day patience timer, the 3-day battle-site timer, or a tier timer.
- **Bring both Noras to me:** teleports both of them to you, wherever they are.
- **Reset relationship:** back to tier 1.

Built together with tiers 2 and 3, since the tier talks it triggers don't exist yet.
