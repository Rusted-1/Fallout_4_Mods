# Nora and Nora – Phase 3 Script (draft 1): Synth Nora and synths

Draft 2026-10-23. Synth Nora's reactions and lines for every moment that touches synths: vanilla quests, faction
choices and Subversion. Reactions come from the "Quest Reactions: Both Noras" doc (your calls) and the design notes;
the lines are drafts for you to rewrite. Each line plays right after the "Synth Nora liked that." pop-up, the way
vanilla companions comment.

Line IDs: `P3-<group>-<number>`. Delivery is a hint for xVASynth. Organic Nora's lines are marked; she only gets a
line where the moment needs both of them.

None of these play in the broken phase (she has no reactions there).
