# v0.6.0 — Phase 2: relationship tiers 1–3

Everything in `docs/script/phase2-script.md` (draft 2) is now in the plugin, plus the optional test tape.
Built by `tools/phase2.py` (records + scripts) and `tools/testtape.py` (add-on plugin).

## Quest `NN_Phase2` (0xB03, "Two Noras")
Start-game enabled, priority 90. Starts tier 1 as soon as `NN_Phase1` stage 100 (Vault 111 choice 1) is done, including on
saves that are already past it. Aliases: `SynthNora` (Synthetic Love `000FB3`), `Nora` (Nora Spouse `001735`).
Objective 20 "Synth Nora has returned to Vault 111", with her as the map target.

### Timeline (`NN_Phase2Script`)
| Step | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Phase 1 choice 1 | Stage 10. Gift timer (24 h), exchange timer (20 h), 6-minute poll |
| Gifts | once per game day | Queued; given when synth Nora is within 1500 units and nobody is in combat or a scene |
| Check-in | after the 5th gift | Scene `NN_P2_CheckIn`; answer stored in `NN_P2CheckIn` (1–4). Answer 1 stops gifts |
| NORA's first talk | 48 h after the check-in | Scene `NN_P2_N1_NoraTalk`. If the check-in answer was 3, NORA's gifts start (every 48 h) |
| Tier 2 | 48 h after NORA's first talk | Tier global 2, stage 40, scene `NN_P2_N2_NoraTalk` |
| Tier 3 | 72 h ± `NN_P2T3Adjust` days after the tier 2 talk (min 24 h) | Tier global 3, stage 60, scene `NN_P2_S3_SynthTalk` |

"Not right now" sets stage 45 / 65; the next time you talk to that Nora, a greeting replays her opener and starts the
"…Again" copy of the scene (`NN_P2_N2_NoraTalkAgain`, `NN_P2_S3_SynthTalkAgain`).

### Gifts
- Types: ammo (the ammo type you carry most of; vanilla Papyrus can't read a weapon's ammo), stimpaks or RadAway, caps,
  screws + adhesive, one rare part (gears, circuitry or nuclear material). Line chosen by `NN_P2GiftType` and the check-in answer.
- After "thanks for looking out": gears + circuitry or nuclear material. NORA's gift: screws + gears.

### Cruel path, hurt counter, tier 4 adjust
- `NN_P2Cruel` = 1 from "dumb machine" (tier 2) or "useful machine" (tier 3 kind branch); "I'm sorry" sets it back to 0.
- Tier 3 cruel branch: check-in answer 4 or `NN_P2Cruel` = 1.
- Every Hate from synth Nora in these scenes adds 1 to `NN_P2Hurt`. At 3 she plays `NN_P2_Leave`, leaves a note in your
  inventory ("Synth Nora's Note"), disappears after 1 h and the battle site becomes available 72 h later (`NN_Phase1` 410/420).
- Tier 4 timing changes are stored in `NN_P2T4Adjust` for Phase 4.

### "That vault was a mistake" (tier 3)
The four Phase 1 choices come back (`Outcome` 81–84):
- **Come with us:** she stays. Nora Likes, Synth Likes.
- **I need time:** she walks back to Vault 111 (`NN_SynthNoraWalksBack`: vanilla `defaultTravelToLinkCustom02` package to
  the Phase 1 vault anchor via `LinkCustom02`). On-screen message plus objective 20 with a map marker. Talking to her there
  (greeting "So. What happens to me now?") offers the four choices again (`NN_P2_VaultWait`, outcomes 91–94). The
  "leaves after a while" option counts 7 days from then and leaves the Phase 1 pod note.
- **Abomination:** hostile, as in Phase 1. Nora Hates; `NN_P2_AfterKill` plays after the kill.
- **Go your own way:** she walks back to the vault; 72 h later `NN_Phase1` 410/420 (battle site).

### Tier 3 combat aid
Tier 3 or higher: when either Nora is below 35 % health in combat and the other has a stimpak (and is within 1500 units),
the other uses one on her, says a line, and gets a thank-you. At most once per fight and once every 5 game minutes.

### Exchanges and idles
- Exchanges about every 20 game hours, both Noras near you: tier 1 coffee / watch, tier 2 left flank / dress / watch,
  tier 3 compliments (8 scenes, cycled).
- Idles (`NN_P2_Idles`): tier 1 synth (6) and Nora (2); tier 2 one each.

## Test tape (optional): `NoraAndNora - Test Tape.esp`
A note "Nora and Nora: Test Tape" given once on load. Reading it opens a menu (message boxes):
Status / Advance to next tier / Set tier 1–5 / Skip current wait / Bring both Noras to me / Reset relationship / Close.
It's a note with a button menu rather than a holotape, because a terminal record can't be built reliably without the
Creation Kit. Status shows tier, gifts, check-in answer, cruel flag, hurt count and days until the next event.
