# v0.4.0 — Synth Nora's battle site (choice 4 aftermath)

Optional add-on **`NoraAndNora - Battle Site.esp`** (light plugin). The FOMOD offers it only when Random Encounter
Framework and all three DLCs are active (REFramework itself masters them).

## When it can appear
`NN_Phase1` stage **420** is done, and **430** (note read) isn't. 420 is set:
- 3 days after choice 4 ("Go your own way"), or
- 3 days after she walks out on the "leaves after a while" path

The encounter is a Story Manager quest node (`NN_BattleSiteNode`) under REFramework's **`RESceneQuestsParent`**:
- That parent picks a random valid child, per the framework's modding article, so it competes with only a few siblings.
- No previous sibling is set, so no framework record is edited.
- The node's own conditions are the example plugin's event-type check, plus the two stage checks above.

## What spawns (quest `NN_BattleSite`, stage 10)
The quest carries vanilla `REScript` (`REParent` = `0002DFEE`) and `NN_BattleSiteScript`. Its trigger and center-marker
aliases are copied unchanged from the framework's example scene encounter. Stage 10 is the start-up stage, and it:
1. Calls `REScript.Startup()`, as the example's fragment does.
2. Enables synth Nora (forced alias to Synthetic Love's `000FB3`) and moves her to the center marker.
3. Clears her essential/protected flags, which Synthetic Love sets.
4. Gives her items through form list `NN_BattleSiteNoraItems`, all vanilla leveled lists:
   - light combat armor left and right arm (`LL_Armor_Combat_Arm*_Light`)
   - medium combat armor chest and both legs (`LL_Armor_Combat_*_Medium`)
   - automatic laser rifle (`LL_LaserGun_Rifle_Auto`)
5. Adds 3 plasma grenades, 4 stimpaks and **"Nora's Final Wish"**.
6. Places an empty no-core power armor frame (`PowerArmorFrameFurnitureNoCore`) beside her, loaded with T-51c pieces
   (`LL_Armor_Power_T51_*_C`, the "C" leveled lists).
7. If `NN_BattleSitePowerArmor` = 1 (default), she enters the frame: `SwitchToPowerArmor`, after a 1 s wait for her 3D to load,
   then a 5 s wait.
8. Kills her. Then places and kills 2 coursers (`LvlSynthCourserNoTeleport`) and 8 synths (`LvlSynthAmbush`) around her.
9. Shows the objective **"Investigate the battle site"** (quest "The Battle Site"), targeting her body.

The encounter scripts don't clean this up on their own (the stop-when-unloaded/dead settings stay off), so the site
stays until the note is read.

## Ending
The note (`NN_SynthNoraFinalWish`, main plugin) carries `NN_FinalWishScript`: **OnRead** → `NN_Phase1` stage **430**.
`NN_BattleSiteScript` listens for that:
1. completes the objective
2. sets stage 1000 (shut-down): re-arms the encounter trigger and stops the quest

Her body and the others stay where they fell.

## Fallback if power armor misbehaves
Console: `set NN_BattleSitePowerArmor to 0` before the encounter fires. She's then found next to the loaded frame
instead of inside it, with the gear still in her inventory.

## Verified (file level)
`tools/verify_refs.py` checked the add-on against Fallout4.esm, DLCRobot.esm, REFramework.esm, Synthetic Love and
NoraAndNora.esp: 0 problems. DLCCoast/DLCNukaWorld are masters only, because REFramework requires them; nothing in the
add-on points at them.
