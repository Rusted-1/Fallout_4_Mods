# v0.6.1 — Quest audit: "Two Noras" and "Cold Comfort"

A file-level trace of both quests against Synthetic Love's own quest record and scripts.

## How Cold Comfort (`p865_COMNoraQuest01`) runs with the patch
| Stage | Synthetic Love's fragment | Who sets it with the patch |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Makes her available as a companion | Our first-meeting scene (`Frag_Met` / `Frag_MetAlone`) |
| 20 | Shows objective 20 "...examine the corpse", sets `p865_COMNora` stage 80 (`Followers.SetCompanion`: she joins you) | Same fragment, right after 10 |
| 30 | Completes objective 20, shows objective 30 "Talk with Nora" | Synthetic Love's own trigger in `Vault111Cryo`: Cold Comfort at stage 20 and synth Nora in the current-companion faction |
| 40 | No fragment; the stage has the "complete quest" flag | Our outcome lines (`Frag_Out100`–`400`) |

Our greetings win over both mods' greetings: quest priority 90 against Synthetic Love's 71/70/30 and Nora Spouse's 70/45/30.
Synthetic Love's intro starts from a greeting, not a forced conversation, so our first-meeting scene replaces it.

## Fixed in this version
1. **The corpse objective could stay on screen.** Our script hid "...examine the corpse" right after setting stage 20, but
   Synthetic Love's stage-20 fragment (which shows it) can run after that. The hide now runs after the 2-second wait.
2. **"Two Noras" objective marker pointed at synth Nora**, who is following you. It now points at the Vault 111 cryo pods,
   and reads "Go to Vault 111 with both Noras".
3. **"Two Noras" never completed.** Stage 500 now completes the quest. It keeps running, so the after-kill and note scenes
   still play.
4. **Two quests were both called "Two Noras".** The Phase 2 quest (it only shows when she walks back to Vault 111) is now
   "Synth Nora".

## Known limits (not changed)
- **Without UCF**, the vanilla companion system allows one companion: recruiting synth Nora dismisses the real Nora, so the
  Vault 111 scene (which needs the real Nora with you) won't play and Synthetic Love's corpse talk plays instead. The
  no-UCF follow mode is still to do.
- At the vault, Synthetic Love's objective reads "Talk with Nora": that means synth Nora.
