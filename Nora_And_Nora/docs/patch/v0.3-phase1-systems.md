# v0.3.0 — Phase 1 systems (Vault 111 choice outcomes)

What happens after the Vault 111 choice. The dialogue scenes that trigger it come later. Until then every outcome
can be driven from the console by setting a stage on **`NN_Phase1`**.

## New forms (ESL range)
| ID | Form | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0x802 | QUST `NN_Phase1` (start-game enabled, script `NN_Phase1Script`) | Outcome controller. Stages 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 410 / 420 |
| 0x803 | QUST `NN_SynthNoraWaits` | Alias `SynthNora` forced to Synthetic Love's Nora (`000FB3`) with vanilla `DefaultSandboxCurrentLocation`, so she idles in the vault while it runs. Flags: optional, allow disabled, allow reserved |
| 0x804 | GLOB `NN_NoraRelationTier` | Nora↔Nora relationship tier (0 = not met, 1–5 = tiers) |
| 0x805 | GLOB `NN_SynthNoraImpatient` | 0 = waits forever; 1 = leaves after 7 days (set by the optional plugin) |
| 0x806 | BOOK `NN_SynthNoraWaitNote` | "Note on a Cryo Pod", left by her on the "leaves" path |
| 0x807 | BOOK `NN_SynthNoraFinalWish` | "Nora's Final Wish" (for the battle-site add-on) |

`NoraAndNora - Impatient.esp` (light plugin, FOMOD "She leaves after a while") only overrides 0x805 to 1.

## Stages of `NN_Phase1`
| Stage | Meaning | What the script does |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | Choice 1: come with us | Ends any waiting, tier → 1, re-recruits her if she had been waiting (stage 200 done) |
| 200 | Choice 2: I need time | Dismisses her (still recruitable), moves her in front of Nora's pod, starts the sandbox quest; 7-day timer if Impatient |
| 300 | Choice 3: abomination | Ends waiting, dismisses and blocks her as a companion, clears essential/protected, removes factions, starts combat with the player |
| 400 | Choice 4: go your own way | Dismisses and blocks her, puts her in the vault sandbox, 3-day timer |
| 410 | She has left the vault | Ends the sandbox, disables her |
| 420 | Battle site eligible | Checked by the battle-site add-on |

Timers (game hours):
- **168 (patience):** if no choice has been made by then → note on the pod, she's released, 410, then 72 h → 420
- **72 (choice 4):** 410 and 420
- **72 (gear-up after the patience exit):** 420

Anchors: `MQ102SpouseCorpseFemaleREF` (`0006FA30`, the floor in front of Nora's pod; Nora Spouse disables the body,
its position is still used) and `MQPlayerSpousePodREF` (`0004A012`) for the note.

## How the scripts are built
`tools/pexasm.py` is a small structured assembler on top of `pexw.py`:
- named temporaries and jump labels
- every call checked against the vanilla function signatures (argument count, return type for temps). The signature list
  `sigs.json` is regenerated from the vanilla scripts with `tools/gen_sigs.py`; it isn't committed (derived from Bethesda's scripts)
- `tools/phase1_scripts.py` builds `NN_Phase1Script.pex` and keeps the equivalent Papyrus in its docstring

`tools/verify_refs.py` checks every built plugin:
- group sizes
- every script property in the plugin exists in the compiled script, with a matching type
- every referenced form exists in the right master and is the right kind of record
- quest alias references and packages

v0.3.0: 0 problems on both variants and the Impatient plugin.

## Console test (no dialogue yet)
Use a test save, with synth Nora recruited:

| Command | Expect |
|---|---|
| `setstage NN_Phase1 200` | She stops following, appears in front of Nora's pod in Vault 111 and idles there |
| `setstage NN_Phase1 100` | (after 200) She can follow again; `getglobalvalue NN_NoraRelationTier` → 1 |
| `setstage NN_Phase1 400` | Dismissed for good, waits in the vault; after 3 in-game days (`set gamehour` / wait) she's gone and `getstage NN_Phase1` → 420 |
| `setstage NN_Phase1 300` | She turns hostile and can be killed |
| With the Impatient option: `setstage NN_Phase1 200`, then wait 7 days | Note on Nora's pod, she's gone, stage 420 three days later |
