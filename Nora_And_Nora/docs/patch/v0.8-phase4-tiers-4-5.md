Written 2026-10-23. Script: `docs/script/phase4-script.md` (draft 2, with the user's edits). 85 lines voiced with
xVASynth (f4_nora), file names 0xE60–0xEB4 (51 Organic Nora, 34 Synth Nora). check_audio.py: all 85 present,

## The new quest
`NN_Phase4` (0xEB5, start game enabled, in the SEQ file), script `NN_Phase4Script`, fragments `NN_P4S_TIF`.
Built by `tools/phase4.py`.

- "Not right now" in either talk: she asks again the next time you talk to Organic Nora (greeting). The tier 5 one
  needs synth Nora within 1500 units.
- A talk you walk away from before answering is queued again.
- "Wait, you two have fallen in love?" plays both replies, then the question again with three answers.

## Effects
- **Reactions** use the same affinity keywords as Phase 2 (Synthetic Love's for synth Nora, Nora Spouse's for
  Organic Nora).
- **"Too hard on her"** clears the cruel flag.
- **"She's just a machine"** starts the broken phase:
  - Broken global = 1 and tier 5 is locked.
  - After 3 s, `CompanionActorScript.SetHasLeftPlayerPermanently()` on synth Nora. In the vanilla script that flag
    only gates affinity events, ModAffinity and murder checks, so her own likes, dislikes and comments stop. She
    stays a companion.
  - Her greetings while she is in your party become "Yes, sir." and the four other flat lines, then Synthetic Love's
    own companion menu (its scene COMNoraTalk_TalkScene, phase "Loop"). The same lines play as idles.
  - Phase 3 moments, Subversion moments and her Phase 2 stimpak barks are skipped.
  - A game day later, Organic Nora's "What did you do to her?" plays once.
- **"Take her out"**: her "I'm a person! A person!" line, then NN_Phase2Script.Outcome(83), the same hostile path as
  "abomination" at Vault 111. If her line can't play, the hostile turn happens 12 s after the choice anyway.
- **"I love you both"**:
  - Duo global = 1.
  - The Eternal Lovers perk on the player (permanent, like a companion perk). Its ability gives +50 fire, cryo and
    poison resistance (vanilla AbFortifyResistFire / Cryo / Poison). In Fallout 4 those three resistances work as
    percentages, so +50 is the "50% less" in the perk text.
  - An ability with +100 health (vanilla AbFortifyHealth) on each Nora.
  - 1 HP per second healing from the quest script (a 1-second timer that runs while the player has the perk and is
    hurt; restarted on each load).
  - A message box shows the perk text.

## Ambient and combat
- Every 20 game hours, when both are near and nothing else is queued, they have one exchange:
  - Tier 4: the two flirting exchanges, in turn.
  - Tier 5: those two plus the four tier 5 ones (two exchanges, plus one line each to you).
- After the duo romance, the three future talks play once each, at most one per game day. The third depends on
  Shaun:
  - MQ207 stage 150/180 not done: still missing.
  - Done and Father alive: found.
  - Father (ShaunRef 0005C337) dead: dead.
- At tier 4+, when one Nora goes into bleedout, the other shouts her line (OnEnterBleedout; at most once per
  ~15 game minutes).

## Synthetic Love's own romance
p865_COMNora stage 525 is where its fragment calls RomanceSuccess. Five seconds after that stage is set, once synth
Nora is near:
1. Her two lines (P4-RO-001/002).
2. Organic Nora's reaction, if she is there:
   - Tier ≤3: P4-RO-010 + Hates.
   - Tier 4: P4-RO-011.
   - Tier 5: P4-RO-012 + Loves.

If stage 525 was already done when the plugin loads, it is marked as seen and nothing plays.
