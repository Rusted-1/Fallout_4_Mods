# v0.5.2 — "Synth Nora" name and affinity reactions

## What changed
- **Name:** synth Nora shows as **"Synth Nora"** (subtitles, companion menu, talk prompt). New always-running quest `NN_Names`
  with one alias forced to her reference (`p865_SyntheticLove.esp` ACHR `000FB3`) and a display-name message `NN_SynthNoraName`.
  Her NPC record isn't touched: it differs between Synthetic Love's 8 installer versions, so an override would break 7 of them.
- **Reaction messages:** Synthetic Love's four affinity messages (`p865_CA_Nora_likes/Loves/Dislikes/Hates`, identical in all
  8 versions) now read "Synth Nora liked/loved/disliked/hated that." Nora Spouse's messages still say "Nora …".
- **Phase 1 choices now apply affinity.** The script's Effect column is wired through `FollowersScript.SendAffinityEvent`
  with each mod's own events: Synthetic Love's `p865_CustomEvent…` keywords and Nora Spouse's `CA_CustomEvent_Nora…`
  keywords. That gives the normal on-screen message and the affinity change, the same way vanilla companions react.

- The Diamond City choices (S1) happen before synth Nora is recruited, and the companion system ignores companions who haven't
  joined yet. They're stored as quest stages 51–53 and sent 2 seconds after she joins (end of the first meeting).
- "Go your own way" applies its reactions only on the final confirmation. If you switch to another choice instead, that
  choice's reactions apply.
- Reactions are sent with the companion comment suppressed (they're inside a scene) and need the player nearby.

## Checked
- Cooldown on all 8 events is `CA_CoolDownDays_Immediate` (0), so back-to-back reactions all count.
- Both companions react once they're in `HasBeenCompanionFaction` (the vanilla handler's condition), not only as the
  current companion.
- Nora Spouse adds its events to the Followers quest at runtime (`NoraFollowerDataScript`), so our UCF Followers merge doesn't
  remove them. Nora Spouse's own copy of the Followers quest is unchanged from vanilla apart from string format and padding.
- `verify_refs.py` and `verify_dialogue.py`: 0 problems in both builds, 89/89 voiced lines placed. All 8 keyword
  properties resolve to the right records.

## Untested
